# Z1 · VALIDATE — Find out what sells before you pay for it

**Owner:** Amoz (stories/lives) · Wife or son (DMs + notes sheet) · **Skills:** `chozen-cmo`, `chozen-social-command` · **Runs inside:** Z3 Days 1–21

> Votes are nice. DMs are buyers.

## The 4 rules
1. **One poll per story.** Sample on a real body, never just a flat lay.
2. **One poll a day.** It should feel like a conversation, not a survey.
3. **Same time every day** so people know when to look. *(Saturdays dark: the calendar tool moves them. On Fridays, post at least an hour before sundown, which is ~4 PM in winter.)*
4. **DM every single "yes."** That's where the buyers are.

## The 15 polls, in ChoZen's voice

**Round 1 · Days 1–3 · IS IT RIGHT?**
1. "Set apart or not for me?" → *Set apart* / *Not for me*
2. "Would you wear this to service AND the gym?" → *Both* / *Nah*
3. Slider: "Rate it."
4. Question box: "Be honest. What would you change?"

*How to read it:* under ~70% positive, or the same complaint keeps coming back? Fix it now, while it only costs a sample. **Hard stop.**

**Round 2 · Days 4–7 · DO THEY WANT IT?**
5. "Who's ready for the [piece]?" → *About time* / *Not yet* ← **the green light.** Every "About time" gets a DM today.
6. "Would you grab this on drop day?" → *Instantly* / *Maybe*
7. Two colorways side by side: "Which one?" → *[Color A]* / *[Color B]*
8. "Fitted or relaxed?" → *Fitted* / *Relaxed* (or two ways to wear it)

**Round 3 · Days 8–11 · WHAT SIZE, WHAT PRICE?**
9. "What size do y'all wear?" → *S–M* / *L–XL* / *2X–3X* / *4X+* (Instagram allows 4 options; run it twice if needed)
10. "How do you like it to fit?" → *True to size* / *Oversized*
11. "What would you pay for this?" → *$[lower]* / *$[your real price]* / *$[higher]*. The middle option is your real price.

**Round 4 · Day 12 onward, repeat · GET ON THE LIST**
12. "Want first access when it drops?" → *Yes, put me on* / *Nah*
13. "Should the list shop before everybody?" → *Yes* / *No*
14. "Drop date reveal tomorrow. You ready?" → *Been ready* / *Kinda*
15. "Last call for the early-access list. You on it?" → *On it* / *Not yet*

Every "Yes, put me on" gets the signup link in their DMs **the same day.**

## The notes sheet (Google Sheet, one row per person)
| Name | @handle | Size | Color | Fit | Signed up? | Source (poll #/creator/event) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The sheet *is* your demand forecast. Their answers are your size split (`tools/size-split.js --votes`).

## Fixes
- **Nobody's voting:** show it on a body, ask yes/no, post at the same time daily. Give it 5–7 days before you judge. Low votes usually means low views, so fill the top with creators (Z2) and events.
- **They vote yes but don't sign up:** DM the same day, link straight to the signup (not the homepage), keep the form to 2 steps.
- **The poll says it's weak:** good, you just saved a production run. Ask what they'd change, fix it on a new sample, test again, or test the next piece.

**Exit gate:** Round 1–2 at ≥70% positive, notes sheet has every yes, DMs sent.
