# Z0 · DESIGN — Make one piece worth wanting

**Owner:** Amoz (approves) · **Skills:** `chozen-cpo`, `chozen-brandkit`, `chozen-cfo` · **Time:** 1–3 weeks before Z1 (sample lead time)

> Scale the winner. You don't need ten products. One hero silhouette, a new colorway or graphic each cycle.

## Steps
1. **Pick ONE hero piece.** Not a collection. 1–2 styles, 1–2 colorways max (24 variants is already a lot for a team of 3).
2. **Write the brief** (Worksheet 0): what makes it different (3 details), who it's for, the scripture behind it, and the season.
3. **Dress the customer.** Plan 1–2 supporting pieces (a message tee at $32–38, an accessory) to raise order value. The kit's drop was tee + denim + belt + wallet chain.
4. **Run the brand-law gate** (below). Fail = redesign, no exceptions.
5. **Build the campaign board** with `chozen-brandkit` (Black/Gold/Silver + one seasonal accent at most).
6. **Price it with the CFO** (Worksheet 0 math): gross margin target 60–70%, kill or reprice under 50%. Floors: $30 tee, $40–55 fringed/numbered. Hero tier $70–90. Price on meaning, not cost-plus.
7. **Order ONE sample** (two if you're testing colorways). Wear-test it on at least two real body sizes, including a 2XL+ body.
8. **Plan the digital companion:** a free "Why the Fringes" study guide ships with every hero; one paid add-on ($9–19) goes on the thank-you page. Use `digital-product-factory`.

## The brand-law gate (all must be YES)
- [ ] Men's top/jacket: fringes + blue ribband on the lower hem (unless it's the athletic/windbreaker line)
- [ ] Women's maxi skirt/dress: fringes + blue ribbon at the hem, modest cut (1 Tim 2:9)
- [ ] Ribbon is a hue of blue
- [ ] Wordmark / C-checkmark undistorted, clear space respected
- [ ] Palette is Black / Gold / Silver dominant
- [ ] Size run covers smallest → 6'5"/275
- [ ] Every claim on the tag, PDP, and graphic is true and checkable
- [ ] Hand-finishing steps are written down so the family can repeat them

## Do not
- Design a full collection for a first test.
- Mail-order bulk before Z1 says yes.
- Let a vendor's stock size chart define "every body." Check it against real bodies.

**Exit gate:** brief done, brand-law gate all YES, CFO margin cleared, sample in hand (or tracking in hand with a delivery date before Z3 Day 1).
