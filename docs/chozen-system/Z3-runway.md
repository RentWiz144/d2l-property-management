# Z3 · THE 30-DAY RUNWAY — Get it seen. Build the list. Count it down.

**Owner:** Amoz (content + lives) · Claude (schedule via `chozen-social-command`, outside Shabbat) · **Starts:** 30 days before the drop. The sample should be in hand, or on the way.

## Generate YOUR calendar (never hand-count dates)
```bash
node docs/chozen-system/tools/drop-calendar.js --drop 2027-01-10T19:00 --window 7 --md
```
It prints every task with a real date and the full drop-week timeline. Saturday content moves to Sunday. A Saturday send goes out **Saturday night, 30 min after sundown**, so "tomorrow" and "3 days" stay true. Friday work must finish an hour before sundown. It refuses ("!!") a Friday/Saturday drop, a window close on or next to Shabbat, and any send it can't make Shabbat-safe. "note:" lines are information only.

**Pick the drop day:** Sunday or Monday evening (COO/CMO): a full work week to fulfill before Shabbat, on a fixed monthly cadence. Tuesday–Thursday is allowed (the tool prints a note). Never Friday or Saturday.

## The rule for the whole month
Before any promo goes out, ask: **does this make them feel more Informed, Involved, Recognized, or Connected?** If not, rewrite it or don't send it. Mix in polls, behind-the-scenes, testimonies, and scripture, not just "buy now."

## Fixes
- **The list is small a week out:** move the date back a week if you can, and DM 20–30 small creators a day (wife/son, from the template). A small list still works. Pre-orders mean you make for the buyers you have, not the ones you hope for.
- **Behind on content:** cut posts, never cut DMs or sends. The list beats the feed.

**Exit gate:** calendar generated with no "!!" warnings, every send drafted and humanized, Amoz approved the schedule.
