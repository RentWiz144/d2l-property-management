# The Standard Mission Card

Part of [[chozen-creator-os]]. Pick the workflow from [[creator-os-loops]]; track status on [[creator-os-mission-board]].

Every task assigned in ChoZen OS uses this structure. A mission without a success condition and a
deliverable that must exist is not a mission — it is a wish.

## Blank card

```
MISSION ID:        M-___
OPERATION:         ____________
OWNER:             ____________
DUE:               ____________

OBJECTIVE:         What must be completed?
WHY IT MATTERS:    Which law, pillar, customer outcome, or revenue objective does this serve?
DELIVERABLE:       Exact file, page, system, campaign, or result that must exist.
SUCCESS CONDITION: The measurable proof that the mission is complete.
LOOP:              Which repeatable workflow will be used?
BLOCKER:           What is most likely to prevent completion?
NEXT ACTION:       The first physical action that takes less than 15 minutes.
```

## Worked example (completed 2026-07-26)

```
MISSION ID:        M-002
OPERATION:         First 10 Creator OS Customers
OWNER:             Amoz + Claude
DUE:               2026-07-27

OBJECTIVE:         Build and publish the free lead magnet so the funnel has a real front door.
WHY IT MATTERS:    Discipline pillar. Nothing in the funnel existed — no lead layer meant no
                   measurable conversion anywhere. This is the entry point to all paid offers.
DELIVERABLE:       The ChoZen 500 Prompt Book — 503-page PDF, 500 prompts across 10 categories,
                   hosted and downloadable; an opt-in page; a delivery page; a lead list.
SUCCESS CONDITION: A stranger can enter an email and download the book with no founder involvement.
LOOP:              Digital Product Build Loop
BLOCKER:           Delivery — a book with no hosted file and no capture is not a lead magnet.
NEXT ACTION:       Generate the ten category files.

RESULT:            Complete. PDF live on Shopify CDN (HTTP 200 verified), /pages/prompt-book and
                   /pages/prompt-book-download live, Klaviyo list WQnsxN accepting subscribes
                   (202 verified), founder post published via Metricool.
OPEN GAP:          Welcome/delivery email flow not yet built in the Klaviyo UI.
```

## Rules for writing cards

- The **deliverable** is an artifact, not an activity. "Work on the sales page" is not a deliverable;
  "live sales page with checkout connected" is.
- The **success condition** must be checkable by someone else in under two minutes.
- The **next action** must be physical and under 15 minutes — it defeats the stall.
- If you cannot name the **blocker**, you have not thought about the mission hard enough.
