# ChoZen Creator OS — the execution system, as a runnable skill

**Claim:** ChoZen's failure mode is not a shortage of strategy but a shortage of *finished* missions —
so the operating system that forces completion is itself the highest-leverage asset, and it only works
once it is executable rather than readable.

**Source:** ChoZen Creator OS v1.0 PDF (dictated by Amoz), rebuilt as v1.1 on 2026-07-27 and packaged
as a Claude skill. Full document: [[creator-os-full-document]].

**Applies to ChoZen:** This is the standing operating manual for the digital-product lane. Installed at
`~/.claude/skills/chozen-creator-os/`; mirrored in the ChoZen-OS repo (`skills/chozen-creator-os/`) and
here in the vault. Invoke it by describing the work — "what's today's mission," "run the offer
validation loop," "write a mission card" — or by proposing something new that must clear the four gates.

## The five layers

**Law** (the governing standard, unoverrulable) → **Operations** (few active objectives) →
**Campaigns** (time-bound mission sets) → **Missions** (finishable, with one success condition) →
**Loops** (repeatable workflows). See [[creator-os-loops]] and [[creator-os-mission-card]].

> **Prime directive:** no new initiative becomes active until an existing one is completed, killed, or
> deliberately paused. This is the same conclusion the research vault reached independently —
> [[double-down-on-winners-not-new-builds]].

## The four gates every mission must pass

1. **Law** — honors the laws of the Most High God, not merely borrowed religious language.
2. **Mission** — strengthens restored identity through at least one pillar: identity, obedience,
   restoration, modesty, family, discipline, distinction, remembrance.
3. **Commercial reality** — creates, improves, delivers, or sells something customers can use.
4. **Capacity** — completable without compromising health, the Sabbath, existing customers, or commitments.

Automatic rejections: live delivery that depends on founder health · another product before 10 paid
customers · chasing reach with no revenue path · generic faith marketing or weakened modesty ·
complexity that does not improve the customer result. The pre-launch tripwire discipline in
[[kill-criteria-must-be-set-before-launch]] is the same instinct applied earlier.

## Current operation

**First 10 Creator OS Customers** — validate a pre-recorded, execution-based product with no live
delivery. Ladder: free 500 Prompt Book (**LIVE**) → $29 Prompt Vault (**next**) → $67 Creator OS
(**in build**). Live status board: [[creator-os-mission-board]].

The ladder's shape matches what the vault already knew: the owned list is the top channel
([[email-list-is-the-top-sales-channel]]), and the price band that converts sits above the bargain
floor ([[twenty-to-fifty-dollar-price-band-converts-best]]). Getting to $300/day means 7–11 digital
sales a day ([[revenue-math-to-300-per-day]]) — which is why the operation is capped at ten customers
first, then priced on evidence.

## Standing rules the skill enforces

- **Daily rule:** 1 idea + 1 post + 1 improvement + 1 sales action. The day is incomplete if work was
  created but no qualified person was invited to buy.
- **Sabbath:** nothing scheduled or published Saturday. Friday closes the week.
- **Health-safe delivery:** a product is not operational until a customer can purchase, receive,
  understand, and use it with the founder absent.
- **Verify, don't vibe:** re-derive numbers from endpoints; missing numbers are *untracked*, never zero.

## Why it exists

The ecosystem diagnosis is strategy A, execution D. A document describing discipline does not produce
discipline; a system that refuses the next shiny build until the current one ships does. The OS turns
that refusal into a procedure — gates, cards, loops, a scoreboard, and an after-action review — so the
answer to "should we build this?" stops depending on how energetic anyone feels that morning.

> **Final operating command:** Complete the current mission. Record the evidence. Improve one
> bottleneck. Sell the result. Then advance. *Don't collect prompts. Complete missions.*
