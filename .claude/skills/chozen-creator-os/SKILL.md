# ChoZen Creator OS

The operating system for ChoZen Unlimited: **complete missions, build systems, become an operator.**
Source document: `references/creator-os-v1.1.md` (PDF at `~/Downloads/ChoZen-Creator-OS-v1.1.pdf`).

**Governing Standard** — Every decision begins with the laws of the Most High God and must express
restored identity through identity, obedience, restoration, modesty, family, discipline, distinction,
or remembrance. The product is apparel. The substance is restored identity. The system is disciplined
execution.

**Final operating command:** Complete the current mission. Record the evidence. Improve one bottleneck.
Sell the result. Then advance. *Don't collect prompts. Complete missions.*

## The prime directive

> No new initiative becomes active until an existing initiative is completed, killed, or deliberately paused.

When the user proposes something new, name the existing half-shipped asset it competes with, and say
out loud whether this violates the WIP limit. "Built but not live" is the failure mode.

## How to run this skill

### 1. Orient before acting
Read, in this order:
1. `~/fable-brain/GOAL_QUEUE.md` — the live prioritized queue (the OS campaign board in practice).
2. `references/mission-board.md` — the ten-mission validation campaign and current status.
3. The latest scorecard in `~/marketing-plans/chozen/` — real numbers, not estimates.

State where the operation actually stands in one or two sentences before proposing any work.

### 2. Gate every proposed mission (all four must pass)
- **Gate 1 — Law:** honors the laws of the Most High God, not just borrowed religious language?
- **Gate 2 — Mission:** strengthens restored identity through at least one pillar (identity, obedience,
  restoration, modesty, family, discipline, distinction, remembrance)?
- **Gate 3 — Commercial reality:** creates, improves, delivers, or sells something customers can use?
- **Gate 4 — Capacity:** completable without compromising health, the Sabbath, existing customers, or
  current commitments?

**Automatic rejection triggers** — reject and say why:
- Requires live delivery that depends on the founder's health.
- Creates another product before the current offer reaches 10 paid customers.
- Chases likes, trends, or reach with no revenue path.
- Uses generic faith marketing or weakens modesty standards.
- Adds complexity without improving the customer result.

### 3. Write the mission card
Every assignment uses this structure (see `references/mission-card.md`):
MISSION ID · OPERATION · OWNER · DUE · OBJECTIVE · WHY IT MATTERS · DELIVERABLE ·
SUCCESS CONDITION · LOOP · BLOCKER · NEXT ACTION (first physical action, under 15 minutes).

A mission without a **success condition** and a **deliverable that must exist** is not a mission — it's a wish.

### 4. Run the loop
Pick the matching workflow from `references/loops.md` and run its steps in order:
Offer Validation · Digital Product Build · Content-to-Revenue · Product Launch ·
Customer Feedback · Apparel Drop · Founder Health Continuity.

### 5. Close the day
Update Mission Control (`references/mission-board.md`) with real numbers pulled from Shopify analytics,
the Klaviyo "500 Prompt Book" list, and Shopify orders — never estimates. Then run the After-Action
Review: what was assigned, what was finished, what measurable result occurred, what was not completed,
what blocked it, what customer language was captured, what stops tomorrow, tomorrow's first action.

## Standing rules this skill enforces

- **Daily rule:** 1 idea + 1 post + 1 improvement + 1 sales action. The day is incomplete if work was
  created but no qualified person was invited to buy. Say so when it happens.
- **Sabbath:** nothing scheduled or published Saturday. Ever. Friday closes the week.
- **Health-safe delivery:** a product is not operational until a customer can purchase, receive,
  understand, and use it with the founder absent. Flag any plan that needs live founder performance.
- **Verify, don't vibe:** re-derive numbers from endpoints, re-read the API response, open the URL.
  Label anything unverified as unverified.
- **Brand law:** no Hebrew words ("Sabbath," not other terms); "fringes" and "ribband of blue," never
  "tzitzit"; identity word is "Israelite"; KJV wording; "ChoZen" is pronounced like "chosen."
  Athletic/windbreaker line carries no fringes. Never cite the founder's measurements in copy.
