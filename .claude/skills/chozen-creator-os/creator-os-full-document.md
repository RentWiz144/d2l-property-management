## 1. What this OS is

The minimum viable operating system for executing the ChoZen Ecosystem today. Not a software platform
yet — the management system, mission architecture, scorecard, workflows, and decision structure that the
future software must automate. It exists to stop idea sprawl, force completion, and connect every task
to revenue, service, and mission.

Five layers: **Law** (the governing standard, unoverrulable) · **Operations** (few active objectives) ·
**Campaigns** (time-bound collections of missions) · **Missions** (finishable assignments with a success
condition) · **Loops** (repeatable workflows).

> **Non-negotiable:** No new initiative becomes active until an existing initiative is completed,
> killed, or deliberately paused.

## 4. Governance — four gates

1. **Law** — honors the laws of the Most High God, or only borrows religious language and imagery?
2. **Mission** — strengthens restored identity through at least one pillar: identity, obedience,
   restoration, modesty, family, discipline, distinction, remembrance.
3. **Commercial reality** — creates, improves, delivers, or sells something customers can use?
4. **Capacity** — completable without compromising health, Sabbath, existing customers, commitments?

**Automatic rejection triggers:** live delivery dependent on founder health · another product before
10 paid customers · chasing likes/trends/reach with no revenue path · generic faith marketing or
weakened modesty standards · complexity that does not improve the customer result.

## 11. Health-safe operating model

Any business model requiring reliable live founder performance is structurally unsafe. Core digital
products are pre-recorded and self-paced. Onboarding begins with a clear Start Here PDF. Every repeated
task gets a written SOP. No customer promise depends on same-day founder availability. Support windows
stated clearly. High-energy work batched during stable periods; low-energy days reserved for review,
approval, light follow-up. Emergency pause language prepared before it is needed.

> **Continuity standard:** a product is not fully operational until a customer can purchase, receive,
> understand, and use it without the founder being present.

Ten minutes, facts only: mission assigned · deliverable finished · measurable result · not completed ·
what blocked execution · customer language captured · what stops tomorrow · tomorrow's first action.

> bottleneck. Sell the result. Then advance. **Don't collect prompts. Complete missions.**
