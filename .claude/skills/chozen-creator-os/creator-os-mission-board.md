# Mission Board — Operation: First 10 Creator OS Customers

Part of [[chozen-creator-os]]. Card format: [[creator-os-mission-card]].

*Status as of 2026-07-27. Update at the beginning and end of every workday.*

## Operation

Validate the ChoZen Creator OS as a pre-recorded, execution-based digital product by securing the first
10 paid customers **without live delivery**.

**Completion condition:** 10 customers have paid, the product is delivered without live founder
involvement, first customer feedback is documented, and the next price decision is evidence-based.

**Forbidden until completion:** new SaaS products, new memberships, additional digital-product offers,
major rebranding, a loop marketplace, certification programs, any feature that does not help reach or
serve the first 10 buyers.
