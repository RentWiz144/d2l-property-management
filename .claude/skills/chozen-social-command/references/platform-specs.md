# Platform Adaptation Specs

One idea → native versions. These are working defaults, not laws of physics; when platform behavior visibly shifts, update this file.

## Instagram (feed / Reels)
- Caption: hook in the first 125 chars (that's what shows before "...more").
- 3–5 targeted hashtags at the end; skip hashtag walls.
- Reels: 7–30s wins; on-screen text for sound-off viewers; CTA in caption, not just voice.
- Link in bio only — say "link in bio" and make sure the bio link carries the campaign UTM.

## TikTok
- Hook inside the first 1.5 seconds, visually and verbally.
- Caption short (<150 chars), 2–4 hashtags mixing one broad + niche.
- Native, imperfect footage outperforms polished ads — lean into the ChoZen scene-generator aesthetic (grain, real light, no gloss).
- Distribution is slow-burn: judge at 72h+, not 24h.

## Facebook
- Longer captions fine (2–4 short paragraphs); story-first, offer second.
- Links work in-post — always UTM them.
- Best for the older segment of the ChoZen audience and event promotion.

## Threads
- Conversational, punchy, 1–3 sentences. No hashtag stuffing (one tag max).
- Post the *take*, not the ad. Questions and bold statements drive replies, and replies drive reach.

## YouTube (Shorts / long-form)
- Shorts: title ≤ 60 chars doubling as the hook; loop-friendly endings lift retention.
- Long-form: first 30 seconds must state the payoff.
- Description first line = hook + UTM link.

## X/Twitter (if connected)
- Lead tweet ≤ 200 chars; thread longer material with `chozen-thread-converter`.

## Cross-platform rules
- Never reuse a caption verbatim across platforms.
- Stagger publish times 15–60 min apart across platforms.
- Every external link gets UTMs: `utm_source=<platform>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=<campaign-tag>`.
- Scripture references stay accurate and reverent — verify book/chapter/verse before scheduling.
